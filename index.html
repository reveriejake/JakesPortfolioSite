About Me

I’m a creative technologist specializing in real-time 3D, VR/XR, and interactive experiences. Most of my career has been spent working at the intersection of engineering, technical art, and production — turning ideas into experiences that actually work in the real world.

Most recently, I was a Senior Software Engineer, VR/XR at ACE, where I helped develop and scale live-service VR experiences from roughly 150 users to almost 50,000 monthly users. Before that, I worked at Microsoft, building HoloLens experiences and immersive prototypes before moving into technical art and production for Azure’s executive communications.

My work covers everything from VR interaction, gameplay and multiplayer to rendering, 3D content, UI, tools, optimization, and backend systems. I’ve also shipped dozens of mobile games across iOS and Android and released several Unity Asset Store tools.

What I bring is the ability to move between disciplines when a project needs it. I can write the code, build the tools, solve the rendering problem, work with artists and designers, and help turn a rough idea into a polished, production-ready experience.
